# Buying on Village City

A short guide for customers: wholesale traders, supermarkets, restaurants, hotels, schools, hospitals and households.

## Create your account

1. Go to **Sign up** and choose what you are buying as (for example *Restaurant* or *School*), give a business name if you are an organisation, and add a phone number.
2. You can browse the catalogue and fill a cart **before** signing up. The cart is saved and waiting after you log in.

## Find and add produce

- The **Marketplace** shows each commodity with a photo, the price per unit, the minimum order quantity and whether it is available now.
- Search by name or filter by range: *Fresh*, *Cereals and Dry Foods* or *Institutional Supply*.
- Click **Add to cart**. Each click adds one more unit; the first click adds the minimum order quantity.
- Items marked **Sourced on order** are not in stock right now. You can still order them; we buy from farmers after you confirm and tell you if timing changes.

## Check out

1. Open the cart (top bar). Change quantities with **−** and **+**, or type an amount.
2. Enter the **delivery location** and your **preferred delivery date**, and any special instructions.
3. Click **Place order**. You see your order reference straight away.

Prices are the listed selling prices plus the delivery charge shown in the cart.

## After you order

| Stage | What it means |
|---|---|
| Pending confirmation | We received your order and will confirm it shortly |
| Confirmed | Accepted and invoiced |
| Awaiting procurement | We are sourcing part of the order from farmers |
| Processing | Your produce is allocated and being prepared |
| Ready for dispatch | Packed and waiting for a vehicle |
| Dispatched / In transit | On the way to you |
| Delivered | Received |
| Cancelled | Cancelled before dispatch |

- Open **Orders** and select an order for the progress bar, items, invoice, payments and delivery timeline (vehicle, driver and updates).
- You get notifications (bell icon) when your order is confirmed, dispatched and delivered.
- You can **cancel** an order yourself while it is still *Pending confirmation*. After that, contact Village City.

## Invoices and payments

- An invoice is issued when your order is confirmed. Payment terms (cash on delivery, prepaid, or net 7/14/30 days) are agreed with the sales team.
- Pay by cash, bank transfer or M-Pesa and quote the order reference. Payments are recorded by our finance team and your order's payment status changes to *Partially paid* or *Paid*.
- Customers on credit terms have a **credit limit**; if an order would exceed it, we will ask you to settle earlier invoices first.

## Reorder

Open any past order and click **Reorder**. The items go back into your cart at today's prices, ready to adjust and check out.

## Need help?

Use the **Contact** page on the website, or speak to your Village City sales contact.
